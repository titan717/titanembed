One embed. A TV player for the remote, a mobile player for the thumb.

Titan Embed picks the right player for the screen it lands on. No ads, no trackers, no cookies — just HLS, MP4 and WebM with captions, audio tracks, skip markers and a typed postMessage API.

    Embed code

    The iframe needs allow-scripts allow-same-origin in its sandbox, and the commands only work for origins you list in origins. See Security.

    Player A · TV

    Built for the 10-foot view: D-pad focus with a visible scale, hidden seeking that adds up 10, 20, 30 and 60 seconds, Back that closes a panel before it exits, and key codes for Android TV, Fire TV, Tizen and webOS.

    Player B · Mobile

    Thumb-first: double-tap to skip 10 seconds, swipe to seek, slide for brightness and volume, hold for 2×, pinch to fill, a lock button, bottom sheets in portrait and safe areas everywhere.

    Shared core

    One state machine, HLS with hls.js loaded only when needed, quality and audio menus, a safe WebVTT and SRT parser, resume, intro, recap and credits skipping, and recovery from stalls.

    Private by default

    No analytics, cookies or third-party scripts. Strict CSP, an origin allowlist for commands and a validated media descriptor that never inserts HTML.
